Privacy Policy — Tadhkir (تذكير)

Effective date: October 12, 2026 Developer: Anas n Contact: anas302233123123@gmail.com App: Tadhkir (package com.tadhkir.tadhkir)

Tadhkir is a personal worship tracker. It helps you keep up with your prayers, read the Quran, recite adhkar and fast. We built it so that your data stays on your device.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no advertising.

2. Data stored on your device only

Everything you enter is stored locally in the app's private storage (Android SharedPreferences and app files). It never leaves your device: - prayer records (prayed, on time, in congregation, missed), Quran reading progress, khatma and bookmarks, the weekly follow-up schedule (wird), saved surah selections, adhkar progress, custom adhkar you add, fasting days, and history; - settings such as theme, language, digit style and reminder times; - images you choose to attach to a custom dhikr. These are copied from your gallery or camera into the app's private folder when you pick them. The app requests no broad storage access; only the image you pick is used; - recitation audio files you choose to download for offline listening.

3. Network use

The Quran text and fonts, adhkar and all app content are bundled inside the app, so they work offline. The app uses the Internet only to play or download Quran recitation audio from the public service everyayah.com. When you play or download recitation, your device requests the audio file from that server. Like any web server, it can see your IP address and the requested file name, as described in its own privacy practices. The app sends no identifiers or personal data with these requests.

4. Location

The app does not request or use your location. Prayers are recorded by you; the app does not calculate prayer times from your location.

5. Permissions

Permission Why
INTERNET Stream or download recitation audio (everyayah.com)
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional daily reminders you set (e.g. for adhkar or your wird)
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-schedule your reminders after the phone restarts
WAKE_LOCK Keep recitation playback running reliably

Notifications are scheduled locally on the device; no push server is used.

6. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. To delete all data, use Android Settings → Apps → Tadhkir → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Downloaded audio is deleted along with the app data. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

8. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read on a standard Android device. Network audio requests use HTTPS.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
